# 52 AI Tools — 52 Weeks

> One AI tool per week, every week for a year. Honest reviews, real use cases, and what I actually learned.

**By [Anshika Jain](https://github.com/anshikajain2006)** · Started March 2026

---

## What is this?

A personal challenge to explore the AI tooling landscape by committing to **one new AI tool per week for 52 weeks**. Each week I pick a tool, use it for real tasks, and document what I found — what works, what doesn't, and whether I'd use it again.

No sponsored content. No fluff. Just honest notes.

## Categories

Tools are grouped loosely by what they do:

- **Code Intelligence** — code search, analysis, graph-based tools
- **Code Generation** — copilots, autocomplete, scaffolding
- **AI Assistants** — chat, reasoning, general-purpose LLMs
- **Image / Video** — generation, editing, upscaling
- **Audio / Voice** — TTS, STT, music, voice cloning
- **Data & Analytics** — data querying, BI, insight tools
- **Productivity** — writing assistants, summarization, scheduling
- **Developer Tools** — CI/CD, testing, monitoring, observability
- **Research** — literature review, knowledge management
- **Agents & Automation** — autonomous agents, workflow builders

## Why?

The AI tool space moves faster than anyone can track. Most reviews are written after 10 minutes of use. This is my attempt to slow down, actually use these things, and document what I find in a way that's useful to others.
